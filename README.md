# Rental Checker

## Group Introduction

We are a group of students working collaboratively on **Rental Checker**, a project developed as part of our academic coursework.  
Our team focuses on applying artificial intelligence and modern software technologies to solve real-world problems in a practical, ethical, and user-friendly manner.

## What is Rental Checker?

**Rental Checker** is an AI-assisted application designed to help tenants understand and evaluate rental agreements.  
Rental contracts are often lengthy and written in complex legal language, which can make it difficult for tenants to identify unfair or risky clauses.

Rental Checker analyses rental agreements and provides clear, simplified explanations to help users make informed decisions before signing a contract.

## Key Features

- Upload or input rental agreement text  
- Automatic clause extraction  
- AI-based evaluation of fairness and risk  
- Plain-language explanations of legal terms  
- User-friendly interface for easy interaction  

## Purpose of the Project

The purpose of Rental Checker is to:
- Improve tenant awareness and understanding of rental agreements  
- Highlight potentially unfair or concerning clauses  
- Demonstrate the practical use of AI in legal and housing contexts  

This project integrates backend APIs, AI processing services, and a database-driven system to deliver reliable and meaningful insights.

## Future Expansion: DocsUProof

Rental Checker is designed as a foundation for a broader system called **DocsUProof**.

**DocsUProof** will extend the core idea of Rental Checker to analyse **all types of agreements**, including:
- Employment contracts  
- Service agreements  
- Insurance documents  
- Subscription terms and conditions  
- Commercial and legal documents  

The future system aims to provide:
- Multi-document and multi-domain contract analysis  
- Advanced AI-driven fairness and compliance checks  
- Support for different legal jurisdictions  
- A single platform to verify, explain, and assess important documents  

DocsUProof represents the long-term vision of transforming Rental Checker into a comprehensive document verification and agreement-analysis platform.
